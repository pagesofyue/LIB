The Maasverse

Sarah J. Maas has spent fourteen years building three series that were meant to read as separate worlds — until they weren't. This maps all three lines side by side, marks the seams where books are meant to be read together, and lays out a path for catching every payoff at full strength.

Sixteen novels published so far, with two more arriving this cycle.

Reading path, for every payoff at full strength

This is the order that lands both tandem moments — the Throne of Glass internal one and the cross-series one — agreed on across most fan reading-order guides. It isn't official; Maas and Bloomsbury haven't published one.

  1. Throne of Glass → Queen of Shadows Straight through — five books before the tandem pair below. Optional as a series overall, but this stretch sets it up.
    Throne of Glass2012 → Crown of Midnight2013 → The Assassin's Blade2014placement debated → Heir of Fire2014 → Queen of Shadows2015
  2. Empire of Storms + Tower of Dawn, tandem These two happen during the same stretch of time in different places — Aelin's war, and Chaol's recovery and politics abroad. Reading one straight through means sitting on the other's cliffhangers for an entire book, so the fan-standard fix is to interleave them at scene and arc breaks.
    Chapter-by-chapter switch list (50 steps)

    Start once you've finished Queen of Shadows. "Nightfall" is Empire of Storms' opening chapter title; "Fireheart" is Tower of Dawn's closing one. There's more than one version of this floating around fan sites — this is the most commonly cited.

    Empire of Storms Tower of Dawn
    Nightfall–5→1→6–8→2–3→9–10→4–6→11→7→12–13→8–10→14–16→11–12→17–18→13–16→19→17→20–23→18–21→24–25→22–23→26→24→27–29→25–28→30→29–31→31→32→32→33–35→33–51→36–37→52→38–40→53→41–42→54–56→43→57–59→44–48→60–61→49–51→62–63→52–53→64–65→54–56→66–67→57→68–75→58–Fireheart

    Finish both, then move straight into Kingdom of Ash.

  3. Kingdom of AshThe Throne of Glass finale.
  4. A Court of Thorns and Roses → A Court of Wings and Ruin The original ACOTAR trilogy — three books, the minimum needed before the crossover means anything.
    A Court of Thorns and Roses2015 → A Court of Mist and Fury2016 → A Court of Wings and Ruin2017
  5. A Court of Frost and Starlight + A Court of Silver FlamesBrings you current on Prythian before Crescent City catches up to it.
  6. House of Earth and BloodStands entirely on its own — no ACOTAR knowledge required yet.
  7. House of Sky and BreathEnds by revealing that Midgard isn't the only world in play — the first tremor of the crossover.
  8. House of Flame and ShadowSpends real chapters inside that reveal, bringing Prythian's cast into Bryce's story as more than a cameo. This is the one point in the Maasverse where reading order genuinely changes what a scene means, rather than just adding trivia. Throne of Glass sits outside the active crossover, but its cast gets referenced once the seam opens.
  9. A Court of Splintered Harmony → A Court of Forgotten MelodyUpon release — the Valkyrie Cycle, read after every seam has already landed.
Want to meet Bryce first instead? House of Earth and Blood and House of Sky and Breath both stand alone — just finish the ACOTAR trilogy before you start House of Flame and Shadow, or its crossover chapters will double as spoilers.
